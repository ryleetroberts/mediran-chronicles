Reading the tree

Each sovereign is shown with the name history gave them, their years, and the length of their reign. Consorts sit to the right, joined by the marriage bar. Caelrics who never wore the crown appear on the plainer plates, and those living today are marked in blue.

Sovereigns are numbered by coronation rather than by birth, which is why Queen Serica and Orrin III stand above Soryn I on the page, and why Orrin II stands below his uncle Daric I. Wyllem's descent runs through Mara Pell, honoring his maternal history.

The line forks twice. The first fork closes almost at once: Daric I left only the infant Alyra, and the crown returned to his nephew. The second opens at Lucan II and stays open for the whole of the War of Lions, until four young Caelrics of nearly the same age met one another outside Wainsford in 731 and none of them came back. Everything from Soryn II the Second Sire down to the present day descends from the one son of Queen Serica who was not there.
