Wand
A scepter of polished hazelwood wound with fine copper wire in intricate patterns resembling musical staves, though the 'notes' are actually tiny copper hummingbirds frozen mid-flight. The grip is worn smooth by generations of use, and where fingers would naturally rest, the wood has taken on an almost pearl-like iridescence. Magic Focus. You can use this scepter as a spellcasting focus. You gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs while wielding this scepter.
Chaotic Resonance. While your Innate Sorcery feature is active, when you cast a spell that requires a spell attack roll and hit a creature with it while holding this scepter, you can choose to create a magical resonance between you and that creature (if it has the Spellcasting trait). Both you and the target make a Constitution check. The creature with the higher result regains one expended spell slot of the same level as the spell you just cast (if it has a slot of that level available to regain). The other creature takes force damage equal to 1d4 × the spell slot's level. On a tie, the creature with the higher Constitution modifier wins. A creature can regain a spell slot from this property only once per long rest.
Sympathetic Resonance. When you cast a spell that targets only one creature and deals damage, you can spend a number of Sorcery Points equal to half the spell slot's level (rounded up, minimum 1) to cause the spell's energy to resonate outward. One additional creature of your choice within 30 feet of the primary target takes force damage equal to half the damage dealt to the primary target. The secondary target is not affected by any conditions or non-damaging effects of the spell. This feature is considered Metamagic.
